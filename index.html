At White Seed International Preschool, our goal is to create a warm, safe, and inspiring learning environment that helps children, parents, and our professional educators grow to their fullest potential.

Using a child development approach, we believe that play helps children develop new skills and understand ideas through their own experiences. Our teachers work to “create the environment” by designing engaging and flexible classrooms that respond to children’s interests and needs.

Our curriculum is developed through observation, reflection, and documentation. We work closely together with parents because we believe that everyone, from every country around the world, can benefit when all people take part in our White Seed International Preschool community.

About Our School

White Seed International Preschool welcomes all families.

Our school focuses on learning through creativity, kindness, languages, music, family values, social development, and cultural exchange.

For students who wish to apply for a full scholarship, our school accepts up to 100 students per year from around the world.

For students who use their own financial support, our school also accepts up to 100 students per year from around the world.

We are truly grateful for your interest in and trust in our school.

White Seed International Preschool

1. We would like to respectfully inform everyone that White Seed International Preschool has a total of 16 kind-hearted teachers. Each teacher has many years of experience, knowledge, abilities, and experience, and understands that two-year-old children need to begin learning new things and are interested in learning about everything they come to know and see. Therefore, the teachers and faculty will provide what is appropriate for young children aged two.

1.1 Kind-hearted teachers will utilize their past experience along with modern technology in their teaching, such as a variety of toys and humanoids as assistants. This will allow children to become familiar with modern technology, learn more, and keep pace with the future world. Young children of this generation may increasingly have humanoids acting as teachers instead of humans in this era.

1.2 Kind-hearted teachers will begin instilling knowledge at the right time, when students are ready to understand what makes a good society while being among their peers. They will also start introducing other key concepts, such as our rights and those of others, our responsibilities, and many more engaging topics tailored for young children—so that a better society makes the world a better place to live.

1.3 Our White Seed International Preschool has plans to accept an increased intake of 2-year-old children every year, and expand to middle and high school levels so that these white seeds can grow in knowledge and capability. We will primarily focus on social aspects, as White Seed International Preschool believes that a strong social foundation enables homes and the nation to prosper with stability.

1.4 We cordially invite everyone from around the world to participate in our project. If there is strong interest in studying at White Seed International Preschool, we may accept applicants for the following year: up to 1,000 students worldwide seeking scholarships, and up to 1,000 self-funded students worldwide, totaling 2,000 students. The executive board will convene again.

Hiring Teachers

We are looking for kind, caring, and creative teachers to join our international preschool community.

• Ability to communicate in English and Thai; knowledge of Japanese or Chinese is a plus

• Preferred age range: 55–75 years old
• Experience in early childhood education is an advantage
• International teachers are welcome

• Creative and joyful learning
• Family values and social development
• Music, culture, and language learning
